Follow these stages to prepare an energy audit.

▦

Room Details

Enter and maintain the building profile and relevant room information required for your energy audit.

SETUP REQUIRED Manage Room Details →

STEP 02

Room Appliances

Add appliances with their room, rated power, quantity, daily operating hours, and operating days.

DATA REQUIRED Manage Appliances →

STEP 03

Previous-Month Baseline

Review electricity bills and establish a reference for energy consumption and cost. If no suitable bill is available, appliance schedules can provide an estimated baseline.

BASELINE REQUIRED Manage Electricity Bills →
◷
STEP 04

Current-Month Monitoring

Record appliance ON intervals and room-empty intervals to estimate energy consumption during periods when a room is unoccupied.

RECORDS REQUIRED Open Monitoring →

Energy Analysis

Review energy calculations and comparisons to understand estimated consumption and potential wastage.

View Energy Analysis →

Calculation note: Energy and wastage values derived from appliance ratings and recorded operating intervals are estimates, not direct smart-meter measurements.

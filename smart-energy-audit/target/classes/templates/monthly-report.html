5. Recommendations

Calculation method

Energy (kWh) = Power (W) × ON duration (hours) ÷ 1000.

Unoccupied energy is the overlap between an appliance ON interval and a recorded room-empty interval. This is potential wastage, not necessarily avoidable wastage.

Estimated cost = energy (kWh) × configured tariff.

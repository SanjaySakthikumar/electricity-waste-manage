Calculation method and limitations

Monthly energy (kWh) = rated power (W) × quantity × hours per day × days per month ÷ 1000. Estimated monetary savings use the configured electricity tariff or the latest bill's average cost per kWh when available.

This is a rule-based estimate, not a trained AI prediction or a direct measurement of individual appliance consumption. Suggested replacement power ratings, costs and savings are assumptions. Confirm product specifications and actual operating conditions before making purchase decisions.
